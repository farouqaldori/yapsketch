How it works

  1. 00:03
    1

    Start recording

    Press ⌃⌥R. YapSketch records your screen and voice, on your Mac.

  2. 2

    Talk and draw

    Say what’s wrong like you’d tell a colleague. Circle, box or point as you go.

  3. 3

    It builds the report

    Your speech becomes text, and Jev links each thing you said to the part of the screen you drew on.

  4. ⌘V
    4

    Paste into your agent

    Your words, with [1], [2] and a picture for each, are on your clipboard.

Recordings stay on your Mac, unless you turn on Jev or a cloud speech model.

Your agent can dig deeper.

With the yapsketch CLI, your agent can look closer: the exact frames around any moment you marked, plus what you said and the full video.
