How it works

No magic. Here’s what happens.

  1. 00:03
    1

    You record

    Press ⌃⌥R. YapSketch records your screen and your voice, on your Mac.

  2. 2

    You talk and mark

    Say what you mean, the way you’d tell a colleague. Circle, box or point at what you’re talking about.

  3. 3

    It lines things up

    Your speech becomes text. Jev matches each thing you said to what you marked and picks the clearest moment for its picture.

  4. ⌘V
    4

    You paste

    Your words with [1], [2] and a picture for each are on your clipboard. Paste them into your agent.

Recordings stay in ~/Movies/YapSketch. Words and pictures only leave your Mac if you turn on Jev or a cloud speech model, and then only to the provider you picked.

For your agent

There’s a CLI too.

The paste is the short version. The yapsketch command, installed with the app, gives your agent the rest: when you said each thing, what you drew and on which page, the full video and the transcript.

yapsketch latest
the newest recording
yapsketch show je89
its full report, moment by moment
yapsketch list
recent recordings and their ids
yapsketch path je89
the folder: video, transcript, every event
